Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short

  • Leafstep needs no account and we run no server of our own. Your progress, pollen and settings stay on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes device information, your IP address and how you interact with ads. Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is used, and ads are personalised across apps, only if you allow tracking.
  • Apple takes the payment. A record of each purchase goes to RevenueCat under a random ID that does not identify you.
  • For the panda’s notes, only the round’s numbers go to Apple Intelligence. We never see them.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Who is responsible

Leafstep is made by an independent developer, Emrecan Öztürk, who is the data controller for the processing described here (“we”, “us”). Contact: info@emrecanozturk.com.

This page is also our information notice under Article 10 of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and it covers the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, open levels, your Daily Sprout streak and results), your pollen, looks and the purchases already credited to you, and your settings (sound, reminders, the panda’s notes) are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They never reach us. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer), which stays between you and Apple.

Your answers to the ad consent questions are also kept on your device, where Google’s software reads them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Leafstep is free and ads pay for it: now and then a full-screen ad between levels (never in the first levels, never for supporters), and rewarded ads you choose to watch for pollen or a hint. They are served by Google AdMob through Google’s software inside the app (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform). Ads are limited to content rated at most T (Teen).

To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same one and prevent fraud, Google’s software collects:

  • device identifiers: identifiers tied to the app or the developer and, only if you allow tracking, Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (city or region, not GPS);
  • device and app details such as model, iOS version, language and app version;
  • the ads you were shown and how you interacted with them, and basic app interactions such as launches and taps;
  • performance and crash data from Google’s software.

Your choices. When the game asks, iOS shows Apple’s tracking prompt. If you allow tracking, Google may use the IDFA to personalise and measure ads across apps. If you don’t, ads are still shown, but not personalised with that identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form comes first. You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and, where it is shown, in the game under About → Ad privacy settings.

Google also processes this data for its own purposes, as an independent controller, under the Google Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

The Supporter pack and the pollen packs are bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your card, your name or your Apple Account.

To verify purchases and keep sales figures, the app sends a record of each purchase to RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), which processes it on our behalf. The record contains:

  • a random ID that RevenueCat’s software creates and keeps on your device (reinstalling the app creates a new one);
  • the purchase: product, transaction numbers, date, price and currency, App Store country and Apple’s signed transaction data;
  • device and app details: iOS version, app and SDK version, language and region;
  • whether you allowed tracking (only the answer, not the identifier).

As with any internet connection, your IP address reaches RevenueCat’s servers. We send RevenueCat no name, email address, advertising identifier or game progress. Without an account, we cannot tell who is behind a random ID.

The panda’s notes (Apple Intelligence)

When this feature is on, the short note after a level and the short verse added to your Daily Sprout share text are written by Apple Intelligence: first on your device and, if needed, on Apple’s Private Cloud Compute servers. Only the round’s numbers are sent (taps, undos, time, hints, restarts, stars, your streak, the world’s and creature’s names and the game’s language). Nothing about you, no account and no location is sent. According to Apple, Private Cloud Compute uses a request only to answer it and does not store it. We never see or store these requests. Lines written by the model are marked ✦. You can turn the feature off in About → Panda’s notes.

Reminders, sharing and clips

  • Daily Sprout reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. There is no push server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications.
  • When you share a result or a growth clip, it goes only to the app you pick. Clips are made on your device and saved to Photos only when you ask. For that, iOS asks for permission to add to Photos, not to read your library.
  • Requests to rate the app are shown by iOS, and your rating goes to Apple.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, what you write and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and to handle requests about your rights, and we delete it no later than one year after the matter is closed.

What Apple shares with developers

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us crash reports and aggregate usage statistics that do not identify you. We use them only to fix problems.

The data is collected electronically: automatically by Google’s and RevenueCat’s software in the app, and by email when you write to us.

Who receives it, and transfers abroad

  • Google LLC and its advertising partners: for ads, as described above.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: the purchase record, processed on our behalf.
  • Apple: payments and Apple Intelligence requests, under Apple’s own responsibility.
  • Public authorities, only where the law requires it.

Google and RevenueCat process data on servers in the United States and other countries; RevenueCat stores it with Amazon Web Services in the United States. This is a transfer abroad under KVKK Article 9 and, for users in the EEA and the UK, a transfer outside those regions. These transfers are made under KVKK Article 9 and Chapter V of the GDPR, using the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers and, for personalised advertising, your explicit consent.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the app.
  • Google: as set out in Google’s retention policy.
  • RevenueCat: while we use the service, plus any period RevenueCat needs for its own legal obligations. We ask RevenueCat to delete a record when you request it, and all records if the app is withdrawn.
  • Emails: no later than one year after the matter is closed.

Your rights

In Türkiye (KVKK Article 11)

You can ask us whether your personal data is processed and, if so, for information about it; learn the purpose and whether the data is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; ask for incomplete or wrong data to be corrected and for data to be deleted or destroyed under Article 7, and for these steps to be passed on to those third parties; object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and erase your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

How to make a request

Write to info@emrecanozturk.com. We answer within 30 days, free of charge as a rule. Because there is no account, we cannot recognise you: to find a purchase record at RevenueCat, we may ask for the order ID on the receipt Apple emailed you. For data Google holds about your device, you can turn off tracking and use Google’s own tools. In Türkiye, if your request is refused, the answer is insufficient or you get no answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Article 14).

US state privacy laws

We do not sell personal information for money. If you allow tracking, Google may use device identifiers for targeted advertising, which some US state laws (California’s, for example) treat as “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. You can opt out at any time: turn off tracking for Leafstep in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and, where it is shown, use About → Ad privacy settings. Depending on your state, you may also have the right to know, access, correct or delete your personal information: write to us. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

Leafstep is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store’s Kids category. Ads are limited to content rated at most T (Teen). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal data (for example by email), write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Leafstep’i bağımsız bir geliştirici olan Emrecan Öztürk geliştirir. Burada anlatılan işlemelerin veri sorumlusu odur (“biz”). İletişim: info@emrecanozturk.com.

Bu metin, 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu’nun (KVKK) 10. maddesi kapsamındaki aydınlatma metnidir. AB ve Birleşik Krallık Genel Veri Koruma Tüzüğü’nü (GDPR) de kapsar.

Cihazında kalanlar

İlerlemen (yıldızlar, açılan bölümler, Günün Filizi serin ve sonuçların), polenin, görünüşlerin, sana işlenmiş satın almalar ve ayarların (ses, hatırlatma, panda yorumları) yalnızca cihazında, uygulamanın kendi deposunda saklanır. Bize hiç ulaşmaz. Uygulamayı silersen bunlar da silinir. Cihazının kendi yedeğine (iCloud ya da bilgisayar) dahil olabilirler; o yedek seninle Apple arasındadır.

Reklam onayı sorularına verdiğin cevaplar da cihazında saklanır ve Google’ın yazılımı onları oradan okur.

Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Leafstep ücretsizdir; masrafını reklamlar karşılar. Bölüm aralarında arada bir tam ekran reklam çıkar (ilk bölümlerde hiç çıkmaz, Destekçi paketi sahiplerine hiç çıkmaz). Polen ya da ipucu için kendi seçtiğinde ödüllü reklam izleyebilirsin. Reklamları Google AdMob, uygulamadaki Google yazılımı (Google Mobile Ads SDK ve User Messaging Platform) aracılığıyla gösterir. Reklamlar en fazla T (Teen, 13 yaş ve üzeri) içerik düzeyiyle sınırlıdır.

Reklam göstermek, ölçmek, aynı reklamı ne sıklıkla gördüğünü sınırlamak ve sahteciliği önlemek için Google’ın yazılımı şunları toplar:

  • cihaz tanımlayıcıları: uygulamaya ya da geliştiriciye bağlı kimlikler ve, yalnızca izleme izni verirsen, Apple’ın reklam kimliği (IDFA);
  • IP adresin ve ondan çıkarılan yaklaşık konum (şehir ya da bölge düzeyinde; GPS değil);
  • model, iOS sürümü, dil ve uygulama sürümü gibi cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri;
  • gördüğün reklamlar ve onlarla etkileşimin; uygulamayı açma, dokunma gibi temel etkileşimler;
  • Google yazılımının performans ve çökme verileri.

Seçimlerin. Oyun sorduğunda iOS, Apple’ın izleme iznini sorar. İzin verirsen Google, reklamları uygulamalar arasında kişiselleştirmek ve ölçmek için IDFA’yı kullanabilir. Vermezsen reklamlar yine gösterilir ama bu kimlikle kişiselleştirilmez. AEA, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre’de önce Google’ın onay formu çıkar. Kararını her zaman değiştirebilirsin: iOS Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → İzleme’den ve, gösterildiği yerlerde, oyunda Hakkında → Reklam gizlilik ayarları’ndan.

Google bu verileri kendi amaçları için de, bağımsız veri sorumlusu olarak, Google Gizlilik Politikası’na göre işler. Ayrıca bkz. Google’ın, hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı.

Satın almalar (Apple ve RevenueCat)

Destekçi paketi ve polen paketleri Apple’ın App Store’u üzerinden satın alınır. Ödemeyi Apple kendi gizlilik politikasına göre işler. Kartını, adını ya da Apple Hesabını biz hiç görmeyiz.

Satın almaları doğrulamak ve satış rakamlarını tutmak için uygulama her satın almanın kaydını, bizim adımıza işleyen RevenueCat, Inc.’e (ABD) gönderir. Kayıtta şunlar bulunur:

  • RevenueCat yazılımının oluşturup cihazında sakladığı rastgele bir kimlik (uygulamayı yeniden kurarsan yenisi oluşur);
  • satın alma: ürün, işlem numaraları, tarih, fiyat ve para birimi, App Store ülkesi ve Apple’ın imzalı işlem verisi;
  • cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri: iOS sürümü, uygulama ve SDK sürümü, dil ve bölge;
  • izleme izni verip vermediğin (yalnızca cevap; kimliğin kendisi değil).

Her internet bağlantısında olduğu gibi IP adresin de RevenueCat sunucularına ulaşır. RevenueCat’e adını, e-posta adresini, reklam kimliğini ya da oyun ilerlemeni göndermeyiz. Hesap olmadığı için rastgele bir kimliğin arkasında kimin olduğunu bilemeyiz.

Panda yorumları (Apple Intelligence)

Bu özellik açıksa bölüm sonundaki kısa yorumu ve Günün Filizi paylaşım metnine eklenen kısa dizeyi Apple Intelligence yazar: önce cihazında, gerekirse Apple’ın Private Cloud Compute sunucularında. Gönderilen tek şey turun sayılarıdır (dokunuş, geri çekme, süre, ipucu, yeniden başlama, yıldız, seri, dünyanın ve yaratığın adı, oyunun dili). Seninle ilgili hiçbir bilgi, hesap ya da konum gitmez. Apple’a göre Private Cloud Compute bir isteği yalnızca yanıtlamak için kullanır ve saklamaz. Biz bu istekleri hiç görmeyiz ve saklamayız. Modelin yazdığı satırlar ✦ ile işaretlidir. Özelliği Hakkında → Panda yorumları’ndan kapatabilirsin.
